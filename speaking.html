Speaking

8–10 minutos · en pareja · 2 partes · 25 % de la nota. Hay dos examinadores: uno te hace las preguntas y otro escucha y evalúa. Te puntúan: gramática y vocabulario, pronunciación, comunicación interactiva (responder, preguntar, mantener la conversación) y una nota global.

La regla de oro: nunca contestes solo con una palabra. Responde + razón o ejemplo. “Do you like music?” – “Yes, I love it, especially pop music. I listen to it every day on the bus.”

Parte 1 · Interview (3–4 min)

Fase 1: preguntas personales sencillas al uno y al otro: What's your name? How old are you? Where do you live? Do you work or are you a student?

Fase 2: preguntas sobre temas cotidianos (comida, amigos, fines de semana, deporte…) y una pregunta más larga: “Please tell me something about…”. Ahí debes hablar unos 20–30 segundos (3–4 frases).

Ejemplo

Examiner: What do you usually do at the weekend?
Candidate: On Saturdays I usually play basketball with my friends in the morning. In the afternoon I sometimes go shopping or to the cinema.
Examiner: Please tell me something about your favourite place in your town.
Candidate: My favourite place is the park near my house. It's very big and green, and there's a lake with ducks. I go there with my dog every evening because it's quiet and relaxing.

  • Deletrea tu apellido si te lo piden: practica el alfabeto.
  • Si no entiendes: “Sorry, can you repeat the question, please?”. No baja la nota.

Parte 2 · Discussion (5–6 min)

El examinador os da una hoja con 5 dibujos sobre un tema (por ejemplo, different holidays) y dice: “Here are some pictures that show different holidays. Do you like these different holidays? Say why or why not. I'll say that again… All right? Now, talk together.”

Habláis entre vosotros unos 2 minutos sobre los 5 dibujos. Después el examinador pregunta a cada uno: “Do you think a camping holiday is fun?” “Which of these holidays do you like best?”, y luego 2 o 3 preguntas más generales sobre el tema.

Ejemplo de interacción

A: Do you like camping holidays?
B: No, not really. I don't like sleeping in a tent because it's uncomfortable. What about you?
A: I love it! I think it's exciting to be in the mountains. And what about beach holidays?
B: Oh yes, I love beach holidays because I like swimming and the weather is hot.
A: Me too. But I don't like city holidays very much. There's too much walking!

  • Habla de cada dibujo y da tu opinión con una razón.
  • Pregunta a tu compañero: What about you? Do you agree? Which one do you like?
  • Reacciona a lo que dice: Me too! / Really? / I agree / I don't think so.

Practica

Pulsa 🔊 para oír la pregunta, usa el temporizador y grábate. Luego escúchate: ¿has dado razones? ¿has hablado con frases completas?

Parte 1

  • Where do you live?
  • Who do you live with?
  • What do you like doing in your free time?
  • What did you do last weekend?
  • Please tell me something about your best friend.
  • Please tell me something about the food you like.

Parte 2 · Free-time activities

Here are some pictures that show different free-time activities. Do you like these different activities? Say why or why not.

  • playing video games
  • going to the cinema
  • reading books
  • playing a sport
  • cooking

Preguntas del examinador:

  • Do you think reading is fun?
  • Which of these activities do you like best?
  • What do you usually do with your friends?
