Writing (Partes 6 y 7)

Unos 20 minutos · 2 textos cortos. Parte 6: un email o nota de 25 palabras o más. Parte 7: una historia de 35 palabras o más a partir de 3 dibujos. Vale el 25 % de la nota, así que merece la pena prepararlo bien: es la parte donde más fácil es subir.

Cómo se corrige

CriterioQué miranPara sacar un 5
Content¿Has hecho todo lo que pide la tarea?Los 3 puntos del email / los 3 dibujos de la historia, claros.
Communicative Achievement¿El tono y el formato son adecuados?Saludo y despedida en el email; historia que se entiende como historia.
Organisation¿Está bien conectado?Conectores: and, but, because, so, then, after that, when.
Language¿Vocabulario y gramática variados y correctos?Varios tiempos verbales y vocabulario concreto, con pocos errores.
Los errores pequeños no importan mucho si se entiende el mensaje. Lo que más nota quita es olvidar uno de los puntos o escribir muy poco.

Parte 6 · Email o nota corta (25+ palabras)

Te dan una situación y tres cosas que tienes que decir. Ejemplo:

You want to go to the cinema with your English friend, Alex. Write an email to Alex. In your email:
  • suggest a day to go
  • say which film you want to see
  • tell Alex how you will get there.
Write 25 words or more.

Pasos

  1. Numera los 3 puntos. Escribe una o dos frases por punto.
  2. Empieza con un saludo (Hi Alex,) y termina con una despedida (See you soon, Marta).
  3. Usa la función que piden: suggest → Shall we…? / Why don't we…? / Let's…; invite → Would you like to…?; explain → because…; ask → una pregunta.
  4. Cuenta las palabras: entre 30 y 45 es ideal.
Modelo (5/5 · 42 palabras)
Hi Alex,
Shall we go to the cinema on Saturday afternoon? I really want to see the new Spider-Man film because my friends say it's brilliant. We can take the number 5 bus. It stops right outside the cinema!
See you soon,
Marta

Parte 7 · Historia con 3 dibujos (35+ palabras)

Ves tres dibujos que cuentan una historia. Tienes que contarla, describiendo los tres.

Picture 1: A boy is walking his dog in a park. The dog sees a cat.
Picture 2: The dog runs away after the cat. The boy looks worried.
Picture 3: A girl brings the dog back to the boy. He is smiling.

Pasos

  1. Dale un nombre al personaje (Tom) y escribe 1–2 frases por dibujo.
  2. Usa el pasado: past simple para las acciones (ran, saw, found) y past continuous para el fondo (Tom was walking…).
  3. Conecta con One day, then, suddenly, after that, in the end.
  4. Añade algún sentimiento: he was worried / happy / surprised.
Modelo (5/5 · 58 palabras)
One sunny afternoon, Tom was walking his dog, Max, in the park. Suddenly, Max saw a cat and ran after it. Tom shouted, but Max didn't stop. Tom was very worried and he looked for Max everywhere. After twenty minutes, a girl came with Max. She found him near the lake. Tom was really happy and thanked her.
¿No sabes una palabra? Cámbiala: si no sabes «correa» (lead), no la uses. Nadie te obliga a describir cada detalle; lo importante es que la historia se entienda.

Practica

Escribe y compara con los modelos. El contador te avisa de la longitud. Lo que escribes se guarda en tu navegador.

Parte 6. Your English friend Sam is coming to stay with you next weekend. Write an email to Sam. In your email:
  • tell Sam what time you will meet him
  • suggest something to do on Saturday
  • say what clothes he should bring.
Ver un modelo

Hi Sam,
I can't wait to see you! I'll meet you at the station at 6 o'clock on Friday. On Saturday, why don't we go to the beach? We can swim and have lunch there. Bring a swimsuit and some warm clothes because it's cold in the evening.
See you soon,
Pablo

Parte 7. Look at the three pictures. Write the story.
1: A woman is cooking a cake in the kitchen. Her phone rings.
2: She is talking on the phone and there is smoke coming from the oven.
3: The family is eating pizza at the table and laughing.
Ver un modelo

Last Sunday, Anna was making a chocolate cake for her son's birthday. Suddenly, her phone rang. It was her sister and they talked for a long time. Anna forgot about the cake! When she saw smoke coming from the oven, it was too late. The cake was black. In the end, they ordered pizza and everybody laughed.
